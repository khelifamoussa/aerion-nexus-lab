# AERION Scientific Integrity Policy

## Non-negotiable gates

1. Simulation output is never labeled experimental flight data.
2. A claim cannot be marked verified without a registered artifact and integrity record.
3. References require DOI, publisher or authoritative-source verification.
4. Journal metrics and submission rules are time-sensitive and must be checked at submission time.
5. AI-generated prose is a draft requiring author review; the human author remains accountable.
6. The platform never submits a manuscript automatically.
7. Controller comparisons use identical models, seeds, constraints, horizons and compute budgets.
8. Negative results, limitations and failed runs remain in the audit trail.

## Evidence classes

- **S0 — Demonstration:** interface-only or synthetic placeholder.
- **S1 — Reproducible simulation:** configuration, seeds, code revision and artifacts preserved.
- **E1 — Controlled experiment:** physical setup, calibration and raw data documented.
- **F1 — Flight validation:** approved real-flight protocol and telemetry documented.
- **R1 — Independent reproduction:** external team reproduced the declared result.

AERION currently contains demonstration records and a frontend foundation. It must not represent S0 records as S1/E1/F1/R1 evidence.

## Human authority

The laboratory director approves problem scope, safety boundaries, evidence release and manuscript submission. Agents may propose, calculate, compare, draft and critique; they may not fabricate sources, approve their own claims or bypass a quality gate.
